About

Zirui Chen is a master's student at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences. His research explores agricultural computer vision and remote sensing for precision agriculture.

He is also interested in reinforcement learning for autonomous agricultural decision-making and practical deployments of AI systems in the field.
